Description

Dive deep into the mechanics of corporate value creation. This course goes beyond the basics to explore the sophisticated tools and techniques used by top investment bankers and CFOs. Whether you are looking to break into Wall Street or enhance your strategic decision-making skills, this comprehensive program covers it all.

We will dissect real-world scenarios, including recent high-profile mergers and acquisitions, to understand the practical application of theoretical concepts. You will leave this course not just with knowledge, but with a portfolio of financial models you can use in your career.

Who this course is for:

  • Finance professionals seeking to upgrade their valuation skills.
  • MBA students preparing for roles in Investment Banking or Private Equity.
  • Corporate strategy managers looking to understand the financial implications of their decisions.

Requirements

  • Strong understanding of financial accounting principles.
  • Proficiency with a financial calculator or Microsoft Excel.
  • Basic knowledge of corporate finance (TVM, NPV, IRR).
